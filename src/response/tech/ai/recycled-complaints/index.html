# Recycled [AI](/response/tech/ai/what-is-ai/) Complaints I've heard many people argue that you shouldn't use AI for many reasons. However, I don't think it's a great argument, because it forgets that we already brought the arguments up dozens of times before. If we accepted these arguments, to be consistent, we should probably toss out our routers, computers, and phones for consistency. More realistically, we ought to aim to make and use them better as the tools they are. Starting with this principle - it is my understanding that this statement forever holds true: > That which has been done is that which will be, > > And that which has been done is that which will be done. > > So there is nothing new under the sun. > > (Ecclesiastes 1:9, LSB) And applying that, we can actually find the same complaints in the past with the technologies we already hold so near and dear. Here's a list (I briefly confirmed the quotes myself, with a ? if the the attribution is apocryphal - feel free to dig into it yourself if you want): - **It's destroying real art! Creativity is dying!** - **Word processors kill handwriting**: - "It's quick as the devil, but has very little imagination, and no smalltalk." (John Updike; 1983) - "There is something beautifully irrelevant to a culture where the idea of literature is being erased by the word processor while even its memory is less than green in the minds of those proud school-teachers who are currently charting for themselves vast cosmogonies of words and signs in the vacuum of Academe." (Gore Vidal; In Love with the Adverb, March 29, 1984) - **Photography kills painting**: - "From today, painting is dead!" (Paul Delaroche?; 1839) - "The photographic industrty was the refuge of every would-be painter, every painter too ill-endowed or too lazy to complete his studies" (Charles Baudelaire; The Mirror of Art p.231, 1859) - **Recorded music kills live music**: - "There was a time when...around the campfire at night the stores were told and the songs were sung...but even now...the ingenious purveyor of canned music is urging the sportsman...to take with him some disks, cranks, and cogs to sing to him as he sits by the firelight, a thought as unhappy and incongruous as canned salmon by a trout brook." (John Philip Sousa; The Menace of Mechanical Music, 1906) - "An abject surrender to the cold commercialism of the present age" and "a serious menace to cultural growth" (The American Federation of Musicians' Music Defense League; St Petersburg Times, Jul 16, 1928) - **Film and TV kills theater**: - "The movies killed the saloon. They cut our business in half overnight." (A barber in Muncie, Indiana; Lynds sociological study Middletown, 1929) - "Video isn't able to hold on to the market it captures after the first six months", and "People soon get tired of staring at a plywood box every night." (A New Yorker and a San Franciscan; The Wall Street Journal, February 14, 1951) - "It was extraordinary how the public had changed...With the newspapers and motion-picture magazines telling the public the private lives of stars, a lot of the illusion and glamour of the stage were gone...Theaters were full of children...obviously bored with the acts and only waiting for the picture to come on." (Sophie Tucker; Some of These Days, pp. 261-262, 1945) - **It makes you stupid! We're losing skills as a society! It's rotting our minds!** - **Recorded music kills learning instruments**: - "But let the mechanical music-maker be generally introduced into the homes...these same girls will listen...and...lose finally all interest in technical study." (John Philip Sousa; The Menace of Mechanical Music, 1906) - **It corrupts young people! It's a tool for evil!** - **It misleads you! You can't trust what it says!** - **It will replace us and take all our jobs!** - **The supply chain is morally tainted! Just look at how it's made!** - **It's just a fad proped up by marketing! It will fail.** - **It helps people cheat and prevents real work!** - **It isolates us from each other, destroying communities!** - **It's a surveillance tool!** - **It leaves people behind as some people can't access the technology!** - **These complaints on X are different this time, wait a dozen years first (attempted dismissal of this kind of article).** If you find more, please feel free to let me know on my [Discord](//discord.gg/pBFqEcXvW5). I presume there's an infinite number really, and many more examples we've all forgotten and no one quoted. This document too will quite likely be forgotten by the masses as well. In my day, I can lend credence that these were what all the people of today were saying. > There is no remembrance of earlier things; > > And also of the later things which will be, > > There will be for them no remembrance > > Among those who will come later still. > > (Ecclesiastes 1:11, LSB)
