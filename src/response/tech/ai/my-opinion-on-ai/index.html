# My Opinion on [AI](/response/tech/ai/what-is-ai/) # Introduction When AI started to get more attention in 2021 and 2022, I thought it was a funny novelty but didn't really understand the purpose it was trying to achieve. Why are people feeding algorithms with mass amounts of copyrighted content to produce mediocre human-sounding output? I honestly thought it was going to hit a plateau and die out. This continued even until the end of 2022 and 2023, where the more I saw AI being used, the more convinced I was it just wasn't ever going to be good enough to get anything done. It produced half-working code and required you to query it in certain specific ways in order to get anything even remotely useful. Even then, for code at least, you could still get a better answer on a StackOverflow post or official docs anyway. It was around this time when I also saw the legal complications it was causing with deepfakes, image, and video generation, and my opinion of the technology dropped more and more significantly as time went on. I'm a programmer, why exactly are we trying to automate-out programming? This continued until around the start of 2025 as I was looking for jobs after graduation. I was at that time learning of how people tend to get more rigid in their thinking with age (for better or worse), so as a [Christian](https://www.gotquestions.org/what-is-a-Christian.html), I made a prayer to God in light of that: > God, I thank you for all that you continually do. Even though I screw up and do things or think things I shouldn't, Jesus got all of my punishment on my behalf. I ask that in light of that, when I get rigid in my thinking, let it be on good stuff, and not on shaming benign stuff. Mold me like a piece of clay into a really stinking-cool pot, and let that pot be as useful as it can be. Turns out, God was already answering that prayer before I prayed it. From elementary school, to high school, to college, he placed me into environments with a mixture of incredibly amazing teachers and also incredibly frustrating ones, with rampant bureaucracy to boot. I was constantly held to a higher standard than I thought made any sense in each and every one. Why did my word choice matter in papers? If I completed all the required tasks you listed on the rubric, why do I only get 90%? Every professor had their "isms" they required you to follow and they often presumed it was the "one true way" to do something correctly. You know the tale - you've got your favorite professors and the oddballs. What I learned was, through the good and bad, that word choice IS important, but not because there necessarily is one true way to speak - MLA, APA, Chicago, Turabian... they all exist differently for different reasons. The truth is that if you want to be best heard, you have to make your words match the audience. Using sports analogies with a non-sports fan, or using video game analogies with someone who doesn't play them often, are both the easiest ways to cause confusion. Turns out, along with this, the environments I was in provided the exact framework to give me the skills to utilize AI most effectively. God was training me with the skills to use a technology that didn't even exist. So, I must learn how to use them and the technology, and do so in a courteous and responsible manner. # Currently Nowadays, I treat AI ambivalently, as just another tool in the toolchain, like the internet, computers, etc. AI is just another tool, to be rightly or wrongly weilded just like the internet or computers. In the same way as the previous technologies too, a majority of it is for evil (you *know* what the internet is used for). You might if you read this come to a slightly incorrect conclusion that I *support* the technology. I don't "support" AI, the internet, or computers in any other way than using it - and they all have their problems too. Honestly, we kinda just swept all those issues under the rug. I have a comprehensive list of why [all of your AI complaints are just recycled](/response/tech/ai/recycled-complaints) you can find there. That's not to say AI is not bad, it's to say that your computer that you are using to read this is just as bad, and you probably should at least be consistent and toss it in the trash can. More seriously, we should all be advocating for more legitimate uses and creation of the technology, like all other technologies. Everyone should support making it better instead of just leaving it to usurp everything in perpetuating evil.
