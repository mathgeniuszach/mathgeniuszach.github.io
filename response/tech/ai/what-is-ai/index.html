What is AI?

There have been many definitions of AI used throughout the last decades, but I think most of them have fallen out of favor - for example, no one really calls the navigation system on your map app an "AI" anymore.

When we hear of AI today, we think of the crazy buzzword things that make companies go crazy - Large Language Models, Agentic Workflows, Summarizing Text, blah blah blah.

sigh

These specific tools are based on a specific kind of tech known as "neural networks" and "weight models" (Large Language Models and Multi-modal Models) that are crazy mathematical wonders. If you want to review those in more detail, 3Blue1Brown has a good lesson on the topic, so I'm not going to cover it here.

Instead, based on my research and understanding of AI, I've found this to be a good defintion:

AI is a heuristic-based statistical non-deterministic prediction engine for text and media.

Breaking that down:

  • ...heuristic-based... A heuristic is a pragmatic shortcut or strategy to solving problems. Like "always follow the left wall" in a maze. AI systems are created, changed, and tweaked by dozens of different heuristics, that get them close to a desired goal.
  • ...statistical...* Statistics is a branch of math that deals with probabilities. For example, the chance of rolling the same number on two six sided dice is 1 in 6 (6 out of 36 possibililties). AI predicts statistically common or uncommon responses with the according probabilities, based on it's training data.
  • ...non-deterministic...*
  • ...prediction engine...*
  • ...for text and media.*
